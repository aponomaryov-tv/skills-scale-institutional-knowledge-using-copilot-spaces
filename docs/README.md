# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

OctoAcme uses a lightweight, iterative project lifecycle that moves from initiation to planning, execution and tracking, release and deployment, and then retrospective-driven continuous improvement. Teams start by clarifying the problem, outcomes, and success metrics, then convert approved work into a prioritized backlog with clear acceptance criteria and a documented Definition of Done.

Delivery is run through a consistent operating rhythm and transparent workflow states. Work is tracked in a project board with columns for Backlog, Ready, In Progress, In Review, QA, and Done, while teams align through daily standups, weekly delivery syncs, and sprint or milestone demos to keep progress, blockers, and dependencies visible.

Roles and ownership are explicit across delivery. Project Managers coordinate timelines, risk, and stakeholder communication; Product Managers and Product Leads define outcomes and priorities; developers implement and review changes; and QA partners and stakeholders support validation and decision points. Communication stays structured through weekly or milestone updates, templates, and a single source of truth, with escalation paths from team triage to PM, Product Lead, and sponsor when needed.

Quality assurance and risk management are integrated into normal delivery and release practice. Teams rely on unit and integration testing, end-to-end smoke tests for critical flows, CI checks including security scanning, and manual QA where needed for acceptance. Releases require readiness checks and rollback planning, and retrospectives convert lessons learned into owned, time-bound improvements that feed back into the backlog.
